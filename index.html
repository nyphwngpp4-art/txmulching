Why Big Iron

Bigger machine.
Smaller bill.

On heavy timber and large acreage, a purpose-built high-horsepower forestry machine isn't the expensive option—it's usually the cheaper one. Here's the math most people never see.

Compact & skid-steer mulchers

  • A fraction of an acre a day in real timber—dense hardwood stalls small heads
  • Weeks on your property, with the meter running the whole time
  • Struggles past 6–8" diameter; big stems get skipped or left ragged
  • Looks cheaper by the hour—costs more by the job

TX Mulching high-HP forestry machine

  • Multiple acres a day, even in thick East Texas timber
  • Done in days, not weeks—fewer machine-days on your land
  • Purpose-built to grind heavy stems flat in a single pass
  • On heavy work, a lower total cost than the "cheaper" machine

Same 10 acres of heavy brush: a small machine grinding one acre a day bills five times the days. That's the whole trick—on serious clearing, production wins, not the hourly rate. Light brush on a small lot? A compact machine may genuinely be your better deal, and we'll tell you so.
