Privacy Policy

Information we collect

When you request a quote, we collect the information you submit, which may include your name, phone number, email address, ZIP code, estimated acreage, requested service and project description.

AI chat

If you use the website chat assistant, your messages are sent to our AI service provider to generate a response. Chat is intended for general service questions and quote guidance. Do not submit passwords, financial information, government identification numbers, medical information or other sensitive data through chat.

How we use it

We use submitted information to review your project, respond to your request, communicate about potential or active work, operate the chat assistant and protect the website from spam or abuse.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers used to host the website, deliver form submissions, provide AI-generated chat responses or communicate with you.

Retention and security

We retain quote information as reasonably necessary for business records and customer follow-up. The chat endpoint asks the AI provider not to store the response history for reuse. No internet service is perfectly secure, but we use reasonable technical controls to reduce unauthorized access and spam.
